About Organic Vegetable Farming

Organic vegetable farming is a natural method of cultivation that avoids chemical fertilizers and pesticides. It uses compost, cow dung manure, vermicompost, crop rotation, and biological pest control to improve soil fertility and produce safe, nutritious vegetables.

Benefits of Organic Farming

Healthy Vegetables

Free from harmful chemical residues and rich in nutrients.

Improves Soil

Organic manure increases soil fertility and beneficial microorganisms.

Eco-Friendly

Reduces pollution and protects water, air, and biodiversity.

Steps in Organic Vegetable Farming

1. Soil Preparation

Mix compost and organic manure into the soil.

2. Seed Selection

Use high-quality organic seeds suitable for local climate.

3. Irrigation

Provide regular watering using drip or sprinkler methods.

4. Weed Control

Remove weeds manually and use mulching to reduce weed growth.

5. Pest Control

Use neem oil, herbal extracts, and beneficial insects.

6. Harvesting

Harvest vegetables at the proper stage for better quality and nutrition.
